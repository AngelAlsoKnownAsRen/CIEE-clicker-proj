## Artistic Statement

### Targeted Experience
My goal in this experience is to give amusement to the player as they play this parody of running a Cho-han parlor as an auto clicker.


### Design Choices
- **Art Style: Drawn by myself, gives a bit of a crayon feel that makes me imagine some kid drawing this scenario.**
- **Music: There is none, the player should focus on the sound of those coins after all!** 
- **UI Design: Minimalistic, geared towards just getting the job done.** 
- **Props: The Cho-han parlors are the most vital (and only) prop we need as a main incentive towards our goal of becoming rich.** 
- **Animated Objects: Coins, though just there for the money effect. Did not change it from the class tutorial as it felt appropriate.** 

### Game Assets & Placeholders
- **Core Clicker: Dice to give the illusion of you being the one running the Cho-han table for money** 
- **Passive Generator: Cho-han facilities, hire other people to do your dirty work!**
- **UI Buttons: Only upgrades and money for this barebones prototype**
- **Decorations: Background of what I envisioned to be a Cho-han room in this traditional Japanese styled building.**
